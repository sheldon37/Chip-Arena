# Meridian: five street plans for you to approve

Start with `comparison-sheet.png`: all five side by side, each with its four ways out of the camp. Then look at each
`*-plan.png` (top-down, with the height of every route) and `aerials/*-aerial.png` (a greybox from above the camp at
dusk). The camp itself is unchanged in all five. What changes is everything around it.

## The five

1. **Crown Hill.** A hill town. The camp sits on a park shelf low on the hill, with the Quay below. The final court is
   the top of the hill: 14 m up the Hundred Steps, then two switchbacks to City Hall at +28. Doors: raised on Belvedere
   Heights, ground on the Quay, sunk in the Hill Cutting.
2. **The Navigation.** A canal city. The camp is on Mill Island inside a drained canal 14 m deep, so every exit is a
   crossing: the Iron Bridge, the Lock ramp down into the bed, the Low Causeway halfway down, and a covered mill bridge.
   Doors: sunk in the canal bed, ground on the south bank, raised on Wharf Heights. Court at +7 up the Mall.
3. **The Arcades.** A covered market. **The camp starts at the lowest street** (your floor-level idea): a market square
   ringed by arcades whose roofs, with the halls around them, are a second street at +7. The court is a level walk
   under the Galleria's glass roof. Doors: ground at the end of the Shambles lane, sunk in the Undercroft, raised on the
   roofs.
4. **The Stride.** One 600 m railway viaduct crosses the whole city 7 m up, just north of the camp. You fight on the deck
   while the horde moves through the arches. Doors: raised at the Terminus on the deck, sunk in the Goods Cut, ground in
   Arch Valley under the tallest arches. Court at +14 on the Crag.
5. **The Yards.** Rail yards stepping down from west to east: +14, +7, 0 (the camp), −7, −14. They are wide and open,
   with wagons for cover, sheds, a gantry, a covered footbridge, the coaling tower and the Roundhouse. Doors: sunk at the
   main line tunnel, ground at the Bonded Warehouse, raised on the Coal Stage. Court at +14.

**Every exit is different.** No two of the 20 ways out of a camp are alike in their first 60 m. A check compares every
pair on height, turns, width, cover and the kind of approach, and all 190 pairs pass. Each layout has one door at ground
level, one raised and one sunk, and each court has its own approach.

**The whole map is walkable.** Every surface leads back to the camp on foot. The only lethal edge is the Cordon at the
map's outer limit. Every door's building is neutral, so any of the five instances can take any door. The district
around it is dressed for its instance later.

## Decisions for you

1. **Keep all five?** Or swap one out. For example, the Stride and the Yards are both railway layouts, though they play
   very differently.
2. **Floor-level start.** Only the Arcades starts the camp on the lowest street. The others start on a shelf, an island
   or a yard with streets below. Do you want a second floor-level start?
3. **City Hall stays north in all five,** reached from the north gate, so the clock tower always points the way. Keep
   that, or let one layout put the final court somewhere else?
4. **The canal is drained** (puddles and stranded barges), so the bed is ordinary ground. Real water would need a new
   swim or wade rule. Keep it drained?
5. **Density.** The Yards is deliberately open, at about a third of the buildings of the others. Is that right for a
   rail yard, or should it be denser?
