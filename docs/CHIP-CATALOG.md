# Chip catalog — 100 upgrades

Default: six unique choices per draft. Match creators can choose 1–12 in local practice or private-room setup. A choice first rolls a rarity, then a compatible Chip from that rarity; picks are without replacement within a draft. An installed Chip can recur up to its cap. Empty/incompatible/exhausted tiers are removed and their weights renormalize, so these are initial tier weights, not a guarantee for every later slot.

| Rarity | Initial weight | Appearance | Chips |

| Common | 42% | White title/border | 26 |
| Uncommon | 28% | Green title/border | 17 |

Rarity labels remain readable without color. Reduced-motion mode stops the spark animation. UI pins, etched cores and recessed bodies make the selection look like socketed processors.

## Combination rules

- All effects are computed by the authoritative simulation. Clients choose offered IDs and submit input intentions. Guests cannot change match options or install arbitrary Chips.
- Projectile mods are captured at launch for visual identity, including reflection. A Pong projectile belongs to its original shooter's single slot even when a blocker becomes its damage owner. Firing replaces that slot before checking the projectile budget.
- Pong applies only to guns. It fixes speed at 40% of the weapon's unmodified launch speed, removes gravity, disables extra projectiles, splitter, homing, zigzag and returning flight. Its contact bounces preserve damage; arena bounds return it to play. It expires on shooter death/reset/replacement. Timed Block's three-return absorption rule still applies.
- Boomerangs have no gravity and start returning after 24 m of base flight or 0.65 s, whichever comes first. Return speed caps at 60 m/s; life 4 s and travel 320 m. Cover still collides. Refunds happen at most once per original volley; split children cannot return. Reflection cancels return guidance.
- Proc effects do not recursively activate other direct-hit effects. Health drain and thorns use actual health removed, including overkill limits. Block healing, ammo recovery and Counter Pulse happen once per Block activation.
- Melee Block recoil is min(1500, (180 + blocked damage × 8) × defender blockForce) impulse, divided by attacker mass (65), then affected by attacker knockback resistance. The attacker receives recent ring-out attribution to the blocker. Block itself deals no damage unless an explicit pulse Chip is installed.
- Combination safety limits: HP floor 15; movement ×0.25–2.5; attack rate ×0.15–5; up to 12 primary projectiles, 4 extra melee sweeps, 8 split children; projectile radius ×0.4–6, speed ×4; crit chance 75%, Chip life drain 100%, jump ×2.5, attack impulse ×12, block-force ×6, received impulse ×0.04–3. Direct hit and bounce-amplified projectile damage cap at 1000. Existing scene limits remain 100 projectiles / 150 props, and reflected damage remains capped at 100.

## Inspiration and implementation

The original request cites ROUNDS by Landfall and community mods. [Pykess's Card Expansion](https://thunderstore.io/c/rounds/p/Pykess/PCE/) was consulted for inspiration around tradeoffs, unusual projectile behavior, gravity and knockback. These Chips use this project's own TypeScript implementation and art; no mod code or assets were imported. The supplied mage GIF informed the shoulder-level energy gather and one-hand release.
